# Cadences

Owner: **Sales Intelligence Manager** · Executed by: **SDR**.

Two entry points, **one cadence**:
- **General** — fit-based (Hot/Warm), first message about their category.
- **Signal** — high-intent trigger; first message opens with the signal, then rejoins the same cadence.

8 steps, ~4 per channel. Day 0 = first confirmed touch. Stop the cadence immediately on any reply, Do Not Contact, or bounce.

| Step | Day | Channel | Condition | Content |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0 | LinkedIn | always | Connection request (short note, no link) |
| 2 | 0 | Email | Email Status = Verified | First email + personalised video link |
| 3 | on accept | LinkedIn | connected | Message + video |
| 4 | 4 | LinkedIn | connected, no reply | "Thanks for connecting. Did you have a chance to read my note? I'd love to hear what your team hopes to create." |
| 5 | 4 | Email | verified, no reply | Reply in same thread: "Following up on my note. What is your team trying to accomplish with real patient or expert voices? Happy to talk here or on LinkedIn." |
| 6 | 8 | LinkedIn | no reply | "If {{company}} is planning patient or expert-led work, I'd love to explore how far the creative could go while keeping a real person at its heart. Worth a short conversation?" |
| 7 | 12 | Email | verified, no reply | Final email, subject "Real voices for {{company}}" — one last note, leave it there if timing is off |
| 8 | 13 | LinkedIn | eligible, no reply | Final note: "Last note from me, {{first_name}}. … If the timing is wrong, I'll leave it here." |

(Wording above is the pharma version; retail uses the same timing with retail copy.)
No video on follow-ups. Danny may edit any wording before sending.

After step 8 with no reply → `Pipeline Stage = Nurture`, re-eligible after 90 days or on a new signal.

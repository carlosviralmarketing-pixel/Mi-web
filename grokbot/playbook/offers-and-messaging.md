# Offers and messaging

Owner: **Sales Intelligence Manager** (edits) · Used by: **SDR**, **Video Automation**.
Goal of every message: **get a call booked.** Sell the plate, not the restaurant.

## Positioning — "expand your campaigns to whole new levels"

| Segment | What you buy | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Pharma / Biotech | Licensed patients and HCPs / KOLs | Bespoke interactive experiences, patient stories, HCP content — with a real person at the centre |
| Retail | Licensed models | More content for ads and ecommerce (on-model, UGC-style, unboxing, try-on) |

We don't replace the agency of record. We add the one thing they can't get easily: **consented, licensed AI likeness of real people.**

## Offer (the button they click)

| Segment | Offer |
|---|---|
| Retail | **Book a demo and get your first campaign free.** |
| Pharma / Biotech | Ready-to-use patients and KOLs we can license to your campaigns. Meeting framed as: don't get left behind, ease budget pressure with authenticity, peer proof. Pilot scope is decided *in* the meeting. |

Pharma rules:
- **Do not pre-build deliverables** for pharma prospects (MLR / regulatory risk, and we don't hold the person's licence). Show capability in the outreach video with our own examples (interactive virtual assistant, congress booth, HCP site, patient cases).
- Never recreate a real person from the prospect's website or ads.
- Optional for selected high-value brands: the "$300 for you" special (details TBD — see OPEN-QUESTIONS).

## Retail pitches by segment

- Already invested in AI → "keep your generator; we're the licensed human layer under it."
- Burned or scared by backlash → a real person's analog, disclosed and licensed, is the version that doesn't cost trust.
- Regulation → NY Fashion Workers Act, EU AI Act Art. 50, California labelling.

## Voice

Short, specific, about *their* situation first. No hype words. People are people, never "assets".
Licensing is scoped, time-boxed and renewable — never perpetual.
